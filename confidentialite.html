Collector

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026 · Version française plus bas

Collector is an Android app for delivery couriers. It shows how much of a working day is spent without a job. This policy explains what the app records and where it goes. In short: everything stays on your phone.

What the app records

  • Notifications from delivery apps (offers, jobs in progress): their text and the time they appear and disappear. Only apps recognised as delivery apps are read.
  • Other apps: only their package name, to detect a delivery app the list does not know yet. The content of their notifications is never read.
  • Your location while your working day is running (you start and stop it yourself), and at each offer, to measure distances.
  • Your working day: start, end, time on a job, time without a job, number of offers.

Where it goes

These records are stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage folder. The app downloaded from Google Play makes no network connection: nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

You can export the records to your Downloads folder with the Export button. What you do with that file afterwards is up to you.

What the app never does

  • It never taps, accepts or refuses anything in your delivery app.
  • It does not read your contacts, messages or photos.
  • It does not sell or share any data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it recorded. Files you exported yourself stay in Downloads until you delete them.

Children

Collector is meant for adult couriers and is not aimed at children under 18.

Changes and contact

If the app starts sending data anywhere, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change. Questions: yonisitbongalaxiwatch@gmail.com.
